About

I studied Computer Science at the University of Gondar from 2021 to 2024, with coursework spanning algorithms, database systems, computer networks, operating systems, and software engineering. In 2023, alongside my studies, I completed an industrial attachment at the Commercial Bank of Ethiopia, working near teams that run core banking systems and enterprise IT infrastructure at scale.

Since 2024, I've supervised registration operations for Ethiopia's Fayda national ID program at Ethiopost — managing field enrollment teams, checking biometric data for accuracy, and keeping registration on target while staying compliant with privacy requirements. It's infrastructure work: unglamorous day to day, but it's what millions of people end up relying on.

I'm comfortable moving between the technical and the operational — reading a database schema in the morning and resolving a team escalation in the afternoon — and I'm now looking for the next place to put that to use.
